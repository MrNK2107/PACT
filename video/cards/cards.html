PROBLEM STATEMENT
The challenge

When the brain goes dark, the fleet stops

Single point of failure
One hub assigns every job. If it fails, every drone goes idle.
Radio partitions
Buildings, hills and storms split the fleet into groups that cannot hear each other.
Double dispatch
Groups that cannot hear each other both claim the same parcel.
“Relying on a single central controller is a major risk. If the ‘brain’ loses connection, the entire fleet stops.”KPIT Sparkle 2027 problem statement
