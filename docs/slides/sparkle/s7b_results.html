Honest limits
Where PACT does not win yet
  • Battery crunch: 55.0% on time vs 56.8% for the central hub (overlapping confidence intervals).
  • Under radio splits, fencing costs some punctuality: a safe drone waits instead of guessing.
  • Acceptance check AT-05 is logged as FAIL: the central baseline dropped 34.1%, short of the 40% gate. PACT's side passed.
  • 2 of 240 sweep runs logged one loss of separation each (STRICT policy, canyon map).
  • Simulation only so far. Not flight-ready.
