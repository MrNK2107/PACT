Four ideas, one protocol
Every drone runs the same code and talks only to drones in radio range. Nothing central decides.
01Local auction

A free drone scores each open job by priority, distance, deadline, energy and charging-pad demand. It bids only if the payload fits and its battery covers the job, a pad and a reserve. The highest bid wins; the others back off.

02Fenced ownershipcore novelty

A claim carries an epoch and a lease kept alive by heartbeats. If the owner goes silent, the lease expires and the job reopens at a higher epoch. The old owner must stop (zombie-stop). The depot hands the parcel only to the highest epoch.

03Shared order board

Every drone and depot keeps its own copy of the order board, built as a CRDT. Copies sync by gossip with neighbours and always converge to the same state, with no leader and no server.

04Partition policy

When the radio splits the fleet, a fixed rule decides what a cut-off group may still claim, so the two sides never fight over a parcel.
